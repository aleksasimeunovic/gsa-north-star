# Case study outline: GSA North Star

The outline lives as a Figma Slides deck: https://www.figma.com/slides/28xKhjOfLXqQwCovnzk6Oo

Sequence (Aleksa, 14 Nov): an introduction about him, then one big title slide that doubles as the deck's thumbnail, then the storyline in the order and section names of the Before and After stories, and one closing slide on what made it hard (Earl: challenges, reversals, what still needs validation). Earl's three design stories sit inside it: the adaptive application (Welcome back, When he disagrees, Built around Milan), parcel delineation (His land, three decisions), decision support (measures, Ready to submit).

**Principle:** the complexity of the application should reflect the complexity of the farm, not the complexity of the government's policies.

19 slides. Each has a statement and, where it helps, a line or a row of short points. No artifact boxes: the artifact description and what Aleksa still has to supply are in each slide's speaker notes.
