# Case study outline: GSA North Star

The outline lives as a Figma Slides deck: https://www.figma.com/slides/28xKhjOfLXqQwCovnzk6Oo

Storyline, from Earl's emails (16 Oct to 11 Nov): establish the original problem (the Before story: eAgrar and ePodsticaj), then the GSA product designed and tested with FAO, then the limits that motivate the North Star, and spend most of the deck on the North Star through three design stories: the adaptive application that reuses government data, guided parcel delineation (three decisions: a proposed boundary, precision and recovery, the change carried through), and decision support around measures and readiness. Close on what made it hard, the alternatives rejected and what still needs validation. Eight steps becoming an application around his farm is one of the strongest moments.

**Principle:** the complexity of the application should reflect the complexity of the farm, not the complexity of the government's policies.

17 slides (was 27). Each has a statement, and where it helps a line or a row of two to four short points. No artifact boxes: the artifact description and what Aleksa still has to supply are in each slide's speaker notes.
