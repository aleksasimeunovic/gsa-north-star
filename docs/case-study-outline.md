# Case study outline: GSA North Star

The outline lives as a Figma Slides deck: https://www.figma.com/slides/28xKhjOfLXqQwCovnzk6Oo

Earl's brief (5 and 11 Nov): a takeaway headline, one sentence explaining the point, and a description of the single artifact that supports it. No visuals yet. The story shows the complexity of the original service, the GSA work designed and tested with FAO, and the independent North Star, told through three design stories.

**Principle:** the complexity of the application should reflect the complexity of the farm, not the complexity of the government's policies.
