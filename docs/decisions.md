# Decision log (rounds 2 and 3)

One line per decision: what was chosen, the alternative rejected, why.

1. **One application list is the home screen, the task list and the readiness view**, rather than change cards above a separate preview and a readiness drawer, because Earl asked for one connected workflow and two views of the same work made Milan reconcile them.
2. **The list starts from the full set of possible work** (update each animal by ear tag, split the river field, redraw a field) and each answer removes what isn't needed with a one-line reason, rather than only collapsing done cards, because removal is the defining interaction and has to be visible.
3. **Removed work stays visible, struck through with its reason, until the next answer**, rather than fading out on a timer, because a 63-year-old reads at his own pace and timed content fails accessibility.
4. **The open task advances on its own when it becomes complete, but stays open when it becomes awaiting verification**, rather than always advancing, because a disagreement with a record needs its explanation and a Continue before he moves on.
5. **Unchanged things are named confirmations that need his action** (your other fields, your details, your measures, your input receipts), rather than complete by default, because "Complete" on something he never saw was a false status and made "Ready to submit" less trustworthy.
6. **No step count target and no 8 → 4 comparison**, rather than keeping the compare view and the "tested version had 8" line, because Earl said not to shorten the bar by hiding work, and the comparison was written for Earl, not Milan.
7. **Each change card shows its own kind of evidence before the question** (two numbers for the herd; a zoomed map with the satellite flag for the river field; a zoomed map with the strip, its size and the payment impact for the road), rather than one card template with "Why am I seeing this?", because they are different decisions and the evidence answers "why".
8. **"Why am I seeing this?" removed everywhere**; only the river field keeps one hint line ("The photo can't see what you planted"), because that is the only place the evidence alone could mislead.
9. **The herd evidence boxes turn into the disagreement** (last year 41 | registry 38 becomes registry 38 | you, today 41), rather than adding a second comparison panel, so the record and his answer are never shown twice.
10. **GSA does not ask Milan to request the registry check**; "awaiting verification" means the registry checks, and Jelena is an optional extra, rather than two equal buttons (ask the registry, send to Jelena), because the choice asked Milan to know how the government routes problems.
11. **The split line is proposed along the top of the satellite flag**, rather than placed in the middle for Milan to drag, so the satellite suggests and Milan accepts or nudges.
12. **The whole line moves by a large grip or by dragging anywhere on it; end handles only tilt it**, rather than placing two points precisely with a finger, because precise placement fails on a phone for a 63-year-old.
13. **A move that would leave a part with no land stops at the last good line and says why in place**, rather than letting the line vanish with only Undo left, because a boundary should explain itself when it stops an action.
14. **The part outside the flag is prefilled with last year's crop; the flagged part is left for Milan**, rather than prefilling both or neither, because GSA knows last year's crop but the satellite can't see what he planted.
15. **Parts are called top, bottom, left or right**, rather than A and B, because those are the words he sees on the map.
16. **Map labels in plain words and a two-item key (your fields; land the government has registered)**, rather than a five-item legend explaining physical blocks and cadastral parcels, because farmers never needed the terms.
17. **Land register plots hidden behind a toggle on the whole-farm map only**, rather than drawn by default, because they are reference only.
18. **Card maps zoom to the field in question**, rather than showing all five fields at a size where labels are unreadable at 360 px.
19. **Measures show the payment worked out on his own numbers** (8.88 ha × 18,000 dinars), rather than generic rates, so he never does the arithmetic; the estimate block stays separate from status.
20. **Jelena's cases say who owns the next action** (yours to act on, waiting for a named office, waiting for the farmer, resolved), rather than open or handled, because referring a case to the land registry is not handled.
21. **Her queue orders her own cases first, then blocks submission, then nearest deadline, then oldest; waiting cases after**, rather than oldest first, because her time should go where it unblocks a farmer.
22. **Bulk "mark as handled" removed**, rather than kept, because it encouraged closing cases that were only waiting, and Jelena's part must stay small.
23. **Milan's case reaches Jelena only once he sends it**, rather than a seeded example case, because an example that contradicts his own screen confused the story.
24. **Milan sees Jelena's state on his herd item**, rather than a separate inbox, so both see the same truth in one place.
25. **First year and No animals scenarios cut; Not renewed and Missing receipts kept**, because the cut ones need their own logic and sit outside Earl's five-minute path, while the kept ones show the renewal gate and a named task appearing. The "no animals → no herd task" rule stays covered by tests.
26. **Design notes tag only tested, EU mandate and assumption**, rather than also "North Star proposal", because everything is North Star and the tag marked no distinction.
27. **Submit, when blocked, makes "Go to" the first blocker its primary action and hides the declaration**, rather than a primary Submit button that only shows an error, so the next action is always the most visible thing.
28. **Submit and the receipt stay separate screens**, rather than a last task in the list, because research showed farmers missed the final finish button; the submission has to be unmistakable.
29. **Unchanged fields are confirmed with one button and have no "something changed" path in this prototype**, rather than a full redraw flow, to keep scope on Earl's points; noted as a gap.

## Round 3

30. **The list is ordered by status: what needs him, then "Waiting for a check (n)", then "Done (n)" collapsed into one line**, rather than a fixed order where finished cards stay in place, so the next action is always at the top and the list is the readiness view at a glance. Replaces part of 1.
31. **Unchanged things form one task, "Same as last year", each thing named, summarised in one line and openable, confirmed with "Yes, all the same as last year"**, rather than four separate confirmations, so the 4 unchanged things don't look as urgent as the 3 real changes. Replaces 5's four confirmations but keeps its rule: nothing is complete until he confirms it. Anything he changes (a correction to his details) or that needs attention (missing receipts) leaves the group as its own task.
32. **Readiness says "n things left before you can submit" and counts the group as one**, rather than "n things need your action" with 7 identical badges, because it is Earl's definition of readiness and less alarming. Tasks that need him show the status as an icon (text for screen readers); only "awaiting verification" keeps its words.
33. **One header line says what GSA did and what's left ("Check 3 changes and confirm the rest")**, rather than "You're an active farmer" plus "Three things may have changed", so there is one count on the screen. Active-farmer status lives in "Your details" unless it needs renewing.
34. **Removed work shows once, as a struck-through line at the top of the list until his next answer; "May be needed" shows only on the open task**, rather than on every card, because finished tasks now move into Done and four "May be needed" lines before any answer was noise. Replaces 3's placement, keeps its rule.
35. **A task that becomes "awaiting verification" while open stays in place with Continue, then moves to "Waiting for a check"**, rather than jumping away mid-read. Keeps 4.
36. **River "No, one crop" completes the field: his declaration stands, the estimate doesn't change, and "The state may still check it" is labelled an assumption**, rather than asking him to redraw or request a field check that cut 1.20 ha from the estimate, because that punished an honest answer, asked him to pick a procedure and let the satellite decide.
37. **No field photo on river No**, rather than a one-tap upload, because even one tap offers a procedure when No should need nothing more. Kept as an idea for the ministry (EU area monitoring accepts geotagged photos).
38. **Road "No" sends the correction itself: "Correction asked for. Until it's checked, the land map's area counts."**, rather than No followed by an "Ask for a correction" button, the same way the herd routes itself (10). "Change" undoes it.
39. **Receipts list the attached files (placeholder names and dates)**, rather than "Attached" and "Yes, these are right", so he can see what he confirms.
40. **Measures show the amount ("8.88 ha × 18,000 = about 160,000 dinars")**, rather than the multiplication alone (refines 19), so he never does arithmetic.
41. **The herd disagreement says "This doesn't change your estimate."**, because the estimate uses cows that calved, not the headcount, and fear of losing money is what this moment is about. It also shows status and payment impact kept apart.
42. **When everything is done, "Ready to submit" with the button "Sign and submit" sits at the top**, rather than a "Sign and submit" title over a "Sign and submit" button at the bottom.
